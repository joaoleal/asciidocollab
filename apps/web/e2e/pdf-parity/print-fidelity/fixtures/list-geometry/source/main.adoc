== Lists

A paragraph that ends the run of prose before the subsection below it, so the space the renderer
opens between the two can be compared against the space this preview opens.

=== Bullets

.Three depths of bullet
* *first* item at the top level
* *second* item, which carries a list of its own
** *nested* item at the second level
** *another* nested item
*** *deepest* item, at the third level
* *third* item, back at the top level

=== Numbers

.Two depths of numbering
. *first* step
. *second* step, which carries steps of its own
.. *nested* step at the second level
.. *another* nested step
. *third* step, back at the top level

=== Callouts

.A listing with callouts
----
first line of the listing <1>
second line of the listing <2>
third line of the listing <3>
----
<1> *First* explanation.
<2> *Second* explanation.
<3> *Third* explanation.

A thematic break stands above this paragraph, and it is a rule an author wrote: the one the HTML
backend opens the footnote list with is not, and only that one is suppressed.

A quotation is set at the alignment body text is set at, because the renderer has no alignment key
of its own for one and inks its paragraphs at the base alignment.
