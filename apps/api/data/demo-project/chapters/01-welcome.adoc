== Welcome to the tour

This chapter orients you: what {product-name} is, what you are looking at right
now, and how the pieces of the screen fit together. Later chapters teach the
AsciiDoc syntax itself and go deeper on collaboration and export.

TIP: Everything you read here is a real {product-name} document. As you scroll
the editor, watch the preview on the right update — that is the live rendering
pipeline in action.

=== The three-panel editor

When you open a project you get three panels working together:

. *File tree* (left) — every `.adoc` file, image, and asset in the project.
  The left rail also hosts *Outline* and *Search* tabs, covered in
<<collaborating,the Collaborating chapter>>.
. *Editor* (center) — a CodeMirror surface with AsciiDoc syntax highlighting,
  code folding, and multi-user cursors.
. *Preview* (right) — a live HTML rendering that auto-refreshes as you type,
  including math and syntax-highlighted code.

This split lets you write on the left-of-center and immediately see the
formatted result, without ever leaving the browser or running a build.

TIP: You never have to save. Every keystroke is persisted automatically and
synced to your collaborators in real time, so there is no *Save* button and no
build step — just write.

[#roles]
=== Roles: who can do what

{product-name} projects have three roles:

Viewer:: Read-only access. Can open files, browse the outline, search, and
export to PDF — but cannot change the text.
Editor:: Everything a viewer can do, plus editing content and leaving review
comments.
Owner:: Full control, including project settings, the render configuration, the
main-file designation, and inviting collaborators.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*This demo project is read-only for everyone* — you are joining as a *viewer*.
That is deliberate: it keeps the tour pristine for the next person. To try
editing, create your own project and paste in snippets you like from here.
====
